Each pair of half-bars equals one full skill bar. Every skill advances in 0.5 crew-level steps except Repair Rank, which advances in full levels. “Plan quickest route” finds the lowest-Crew-XP combination. “Plan optimal route” prioritises the skills that most improve combat handling, reloading, repairs and survivability while avoiding low-value side skills such as artillery timing. “Plan optimal autoloader” does the same for Ground crews while excluding the entire Loader role. Data uses the current War Thunder Wiki crew-cost table; this unofficial page stores everything only in your browser.
